## Excluded artwork

The previous AI-generated sets `ivory`, `marble`, `royal`, `copper`, and `ocean` were removed.
Lichess sets marked non-commercial, freeware/personal-use, or listed by Lichess under its
"Exceptions (non-free)" section are intentionally not bundled.
